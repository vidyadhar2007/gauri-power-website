Servo Stabilizer 4-Tier Quality Gating Architecture

Every heavy-duty industrial stabilizer built in our Ropar works passes four sequential quality gates. Equipment cannot advance to the next manufacturing stage without signed quality release certificates.

Quality Gate 01

Inward Raw Material Metallurgy

100% Traceable Mill Certificates

Every raw material consignment is chemically and electrically verified before release to the shop floor:

Electrolytic Copper (≥ 99.9% Pure): Conductivity verified > 100% IACS with micro-ohm resistance tests.
Prime CRGO Steel (M4 / Laser-Scribed): Epstein bridge testing verifying specific core losses < 0.85 W/kg.
Insulation Materials: Nomex 410 & pre-compressed pressboard tested for Class F/H thermal withstand.
Zero Scrap / Zero Reconditioned Policy
Quality Gate 02

Strip Winding & Roller Track

Tension Control & Step-Lap Joints

Precision engineering during coil winding and core assembly guarantees mechanical and thermal resilience:

Rectangular Copper Strip (≥ 24 mm²): Verified cross-section for maximum motor inrush withstand.
Graphite Roller Track Alignment: CNC track uniformity check ensuring continuous contact with zero wear.
45° Mitred Step-Lap Core: Precision stacking eliminating air gaps and keeping acoustic noise < 50 dB.
Continuous Rolling Contact Inspection
Quality Gate 03

Vacuum Baking & Oil Tanking

Moisture Extraction Under Vacuum

Core-coil assemblies undergo controlled thermal baking and de-aerated oil filling under deep vacuum:

Thermostatic Baking (105°C): Multi-stage oven drying cycle purging moisture from cellulose insulation.
Deep Vacuum Impregnation (< 0.1 mbar): Vacuum filling prevents micro air bubbles and corona discharge.
24-Hour Tank Pressure Test: Enclosure tested at 0.5 kg/cm² pressure guaranteeing zero oil seepages.
Moisture-Free Dielectric Sealing

SERVO VOLTAGE STABILIZER ROUTINE LABORATORY SUITE

6 Fundamental Routine Tests for Stabilizers (IS 9815 & IS 2026)

Conducted in our computerized testing bay at Ropar with calibrated digital analyzers traceable to National Physical Laboratory (NPL) standards.

01
Kelvin Bridge

Measurement of Winding Resistance

Measured across all phases using a digital Kelvin double bridge / micro-ohmmeter. Confirms precise rectangular copper cross-section, verifies phase resistance balance within ± 1%, and provides reference baseline for 75°C thermal loss calculations.

02
Dynamic Testing

Voltage Regulation Accuracy & Speed of Correction (IS 9815)

Subjected to simulated input swings across lower limit (e.g. 300V) to upper limit (e.g. 480V). Confirms true RMS output holding steady within ± 1% (or ± 0.5% precision) with response correction speed up to 35 V/sec.

03
70 kV HV Bench

Separate Source AC High Voltage Withstand

Applies power-frequency high voltage (28 kV for 11kV HT class / 70 kV for 33kV class / 3 kV for 415V LT class) between windings and grounded steel enclosure for 60 seconds to prove insulation dielectric integrity without flashover.

04
200 Hz Motor Gen

Induced Overvoltage Withstand (DVDF)

Tested at double rated voltage at 100 Hz / 200 Hz frequency for 60 seconds. Stresses turn-to-turn insulation, roller contact track, and interlayer Nomex dielectric without saturating the magnetic silicon core.

05
Microcontroller AVR

AVR Calibration & Electronic Safety Trips

Calibrates digital microcontroller AVR sensing circuits, under-voltage trip, over-voltage trip, phase-reversal sensing, single-phasing prevention, and electronic overload timer to safeguard connected plant loads.

06
Loss Analyzer

No-Load Iron Losses & Full-Load Impedance (%Z)

Measures core magnetizing losses at rated voltage and circulates rated current under short-circuit conditions to measure I²R copper losses, corrected mathematically to 75°C reference temperature.

Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) Pre-Dispatch Verification Bench

As an authorized dealer and switchgear partner for 11 kV & 33 kV Vacuum Circuit Breakers, Gauri Power enforces a rigorous pre-dispatch testing bench to ensure every breaker delivers instantaneous arc quenching and mechanical reliability.

VCB Gate 01

OEM Type-Test & Seal Check

CPRI / ERDA Verified Breakers

Every breaker unit is inspected for OEM authentication, certified serial numbers, and type test validity:

Type Test Compliance: Full short-circuit breaking tests (up to 31.5 kA / 40 kA) certified by CPRI / ERDA.
OEM Serial Traceability: Direct verification against factory master database.
Factory Seal Integrity: Tamper-proof mechanism seals confirmed intact prior to acceptance.
Genuine OEM Equipment Guarantee
VCB Gate 02

Vacuum Bottle Integrity

High-Voltage Dielectric Gap Test

The hermetically sealed ceramic vacuum interrupter is tested for perfect vacuum retention (< 10&supmin;&sup5; mbar):

AC High Voltage across Gap: 28 kV RMS for 11kV VCB / 70 kV RMS for 33kV VCB applied across open contacts.
Zero Puncture / Flashover: Verifies absolute seal integrity of vacuum bottle and ceramic envelope.
Leak Rate Audit: Guaranteed vacuum retention for over 30 years operational service life.
Zero Vacuum Degradation Tolerance
VCB Gate 03

Micro-Ohm Contact Resistance

100A DC Ducter Injection Test

Main current-carrying poles are measured under 100A DC to detect contact oxidation or misalignment:

Low Contact Resistance: Measured value strictly kept below 40 μΩ per breaker pole.
No Localized Hotspots: Prevents thermal runaway and terminal overheating under full load (630A–2000A).
Contact Wipe & Travel: Breaker overtravel and contact wipe distances micrometer-calibrated.
Ultra-Low Joint Heating Guarantee
VCB Gate 04

Timing & Mechanical Endurance

Millisecond Contact Simultaneity

Digital breaker timing analyzer records operating velocities and synchronism between all 3 poles:

Closing Time < 50 ms: Fast spring-assisted closing ensuring immediate circuit energization.
Opening Time < 35 ms: Ultra-fast tripping minimizing arc energy during heavy bus faults.
Pole Discrepancy < 2 ms: Simultaneous contact touch preventing system unbalance and voltage transients.
Class M2 Mechanical Duty Verified
HIGH VOLTAGE SWITCHGEAR VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

VCB Switchgear Pre-Commissioning Tests (IEC 62271-100)

Prior to dispatch to client industrial plants and electrical substations, every 11kV & 33kV VCB panel undergoes standardized functional verification:

A
Vacuum Hi-Pot

Vacuum Bottle Dielectric Integrity Test

Applies rated power-frequency AC high voltage (28 kV for 11kV class / 70 kV for 33kV class) across open vacuum contacts for 60 seconds. Guarantees that the vacuum chamber has zero leakage and maintains rated arc extinguishing capacity.

B
Millisecond Timer

Operating Timing & Contact Simultaneity

Using a precision multi-channel timing analyzer, breaker closing time (< 50 ms), opening time (< 35 ms), close-open trip cycle, and contact pole synchronism (< 2 ms discrepancy) are recorded and verified.

C
100A DC Ducter

Contact Resistance Measurement (≤ 40 μΩ)

100A DC micro-ohmmeter injection across main copper-chromium vacuum contacts and primary tulip disconnecting contacts. Confirms contact resistance ≤ 40 micro-ohms to prevent thermal hotspots under continuous operation.

D
Secondary Injection

Numerical Protection Relay Calibration

Calibrates microprocessor protection relays (Overcurrent 50/51, Earth Fault 50N/51N, Instantaneous Trip) via secondary current injection kits to verify trip curves, pick-up thresholds, and breaker tripping response.

E
Interlock Bench

Mechanical Safety & Anti-Pumping Scheme

100% functional testing of draw-out truck racking interlocks (Service / Test / Disconnected positions), door interlocks, earthing switch interlocking, spring charging motor operation, and electrical anti-pumping circuits.

F
1 kV Megger

Auxiliary Circuit Insulation & Wiring Audit

1000V DC Megger insulation resistance test between control wiring and earth. Verifies 2 kV power frequency dielectric withstand on all CT/PT secondary circuits, trip coils, close coils, and control terminal blocks.
