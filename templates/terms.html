Commercial Terms & Conditions

Standard Industrial Sales Terms | Gauri Power

Important System Rule: A Request for Quotation (RFQ) submitted through this portal does not constitute a binding purchase contract or order. Official binding prices and delivery schedules are only established when an authorized quotation is formally issued by Gauri Power and accepted in writing.

1. Quotation Validity & Pricing

All official quotations issued by Gauri Power remain valid for the period stipulated in the quotation document (normally 30 calendar days from issue date), subject to base copper and CRGO steel market index adjustments where expressly noted.

2. Taxes, Duties & Freight

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in the commercial quotation breakdown, Goods & Services Tax (GST) is charged extra at statutory prevailing rates (currently 18% for electrical machinery). Freight, transit insurance, and unloading at site are billed as specified in the quotation terms.

3. 5-Year Standard Warranty Terms

Gauri Power automatic servo voltage stabilizers and switchgear systems carry a comprehensive 5-Year Warranty in India against defective materials and manufacturing workmanship under normal rated operating conditions and continuous duty cycle. Warranty excludes unauthorized modifications or physical external damage.

4. Inspection & Testing

Factory routine tests are performed free of charge. Client or third-party inspection (TPIA) at our Ropar works must be requested at the time of purchase order confirmation.
