Mandatory 100% Routine Factory Testing Protocols

Prior to dispatch approval, every manufactured Servo Stabilizer and supplied VCB panel must pass 100% of the following routine electrical tests in our computerized test bay at Ropar. All parameters are permanently logged in the unit's official Factory Test Certificate (FTC).

A. Servo Voltage Stabilizer Routine Testing Suite (IS 9815 & IS 2026)

In-house laboratory verification at Ropar Works prior to oil tank sealing & dispatch

IS 9815 Certified
# Routine Test Parameter Engineering Methodology & Equipment Standard Acceptance Criteria
01
Voltage Regulation Accuracy & Speed (IS 9815)
Dynamic input fluctuation simulation 		Simulated variable line voltage applied across input swing (e.g. 300V–460V); output monitored via true RMS calibrated digital analyzers. Output held steady within ± 1% (or ± 0.5%); correction speed up to 35 V/sec.
02
Dielectric High Voltage Breakdown Test (HV Test)
Separate source AC test for 60 seconds 		Applied voltage of 28 kV (11kV class) / 70 kV (33kV class) / 3 kV (415V class) between windings and ground using calibrated high-voltage test bench. Zero dielectric puncture, zero flashover, leakage current within IS 9815 limits.
03
Induced Over-Voltage Withstand Test (DVDF)
Double Voltage & Double Frequency 		Tested at 100 Hz / 200 Hz at double the rated voltage for 60 seconds to verify inter-turn and inter-layer insulation strength without core saturation. No insulation puncture across turns, layers, or roller contact tracks.
04
Insulation Resistance (Megger Test)
HV to Earth, LV to Earth & HV to LV 		Measured using calibrated 2500 V / 5000 V digital motor-driven insulation tester following vacuum thermostatic oven drying. Minimum ≥ 1000 MΩ at ambient temperature (exceeds IS specs).
05
Winding Resistance & Phase Balance
All phases verified on Kelvin Bridge 		Measured using precision digital Kelvin double bridge; confirms copper strip cross-section (≥ 24 mm²) and balance. Phase-to-phase resistance balance within ± 1% design tolerance.
06
No-Load Iron Loss & Full-Load Losses (%Z)
Core magnetization & copper loss 		Measured at rated voltage and circulates rated current under short-circuit conditions; corrected to 75°C operating reference. Ultra-low loss performance complying with guaranteed efficiency (> 99%).
07
Insulating Oil Dielectric Strength (BDV)
Virgin mineral oil test as per IS 335 		Oil tested in motorized test cell with 2.5 mm spherical electrodes across 6 successive breakdown cycles prior to tank vacuum filling. Breakdown voltage > 60 kV (min 30 kV standard); moisture content < 15 PPM.

B. Vacuum Circuit Breaker Pre-Dispatch Verification Bench (IEC 62271-100 & IS 13118)

Authorized dealership testing on every 11kV & 33kV breaker unit before site delivery

IEC 62271-100 Compliant
# Routine Test Parameter Engineering Methodology & Equipment Standard Acceptance Criteria
01
Vacuum Bottle Dielectric Gap Integrity
Power frequency withstand across open gap 		Applies rated AC high voltage (28 kV for 11kV / 70 kV for 33kV) across open vacuum contacts for 60 seconds. Zero breakdown, zero corona discharge; proves 100% vacuum seal retention.
02
Micro-Ohm Contact Resistance (Ducter Test)
100A DC digital injection across main poles 		Direct current of 100A DC injected across vacuum contacts and tulip isolating terminals using a digital micro-ohmmeter. Contact resistance strictly ≤ 40 μΩ per pole (prevents thermal hotspots).
03
Operating Timing & Contact Simultaneity
Millisecond multi-channel timer analyzer 		Digital timer records closing time, opening time, close-open trip cycle, and contact synchronism between all 3 poles. Closing < 50 ms; opening < 35 ms; pole discrepancy < 2 ms.
04
Numerical Protection Relay Calibration
Secondary current injection testing 		Secondary injection kit inputs calibrated fault currents into microprocessor relays to verify 50/51 and 50N/51N curves. Accurate trip timing within ± 5% of programmed relay curves.
05
Mechanical Interlocking & Anti-Pumping
Draw-out truck safety interlocking 		Mechanical racking verification across Service, Test, and Disconnected positions; door interlocks and anti-pumping relays. 100% fail-safe interlocking preventing improper breaker operation.
06
Auxiliary Circuit & Motor Insulation
1000V DC Megger & 2 kV AC dielectric 		Insulation resistance measured on spring charging motors, trip/close coils, and control terminal wiring. Megger ≥ 100 MΩ; 2 kV power frequency dielectric withstand for 60s.
