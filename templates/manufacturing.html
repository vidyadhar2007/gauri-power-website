STAGE 01 OF 07 Engineering Design & Sizing Bay

Custom Voltage Window Modeling & Buck-Boost Electromagnetic Design

Every Gauri Power industrial servo stabilizer begins with customized computerized modeling of the client's plant conditions. Rather than using generic off-the-shelf dimmers, our design engineers calculate exact turn-by-turn buck-boost voltage ratios, radial short-circuit forces, radiator convection surface requirements, and dynamic inrush currents matching your plant's equipment load profile.

Flux Density Optimization: Operating magnetic flux kept strictly below 1.65 Tesla, preventing core saturation and current surges during extreme high voltage swings (up to 480V/500V).
Buck-Boost Ratio Calculation: High-capacity series transformer custom-proportioned to handle the exact symmetrical or asymmetrical input swing (e.g., 340V–460V, 300V–460V, or 280V–500V).
Dynamic Short-Circuit Bracing: Clamping structural channels engineered to withstand momentary line faults up to 25 times rated current without physical coil displacement.

STAGE 02 OF 07 Magnetic Core Stacking Bay

Prime CRGO Core Slitting with 45° Mitred Step-Lap Joints

The internal magnetic core forms the active inductive circuit of both the series buck-boost transformer and the vertical regulating auto-transformer column. Gauri Power strictly utilizes 100% prime-grade Cold Rolled Grain Oriented (CRGO) high-permeability silicon steel (M4, 23ZH90, or laser-scribed grades). We have an absolute zero-tolerance policy against recycled scrap or local re-stamped sheet.

45° Step-Lap Mitred Stacking: Minimizes air gaps at corner joints, slashing idle magnetizing current and reducing acoustic humming below 50 dB.
Non-Magnetic Structural Clamping: High-tensile tie rods equipped with insulating bakelite collars eliminate stray eddy-current loops and prevent heating.
Epoxy Edge Bonding: Core limbs receive an anti-corrosive high-temperature epoxy coating to protect against vibration, dust, and humidity.

STAGE 03 OF 07 Heavy Copper Winding & Roller Bay

Rectangular Copper Strip Winding (≥ 24 mm²) with Graphite Rolling Contacts

This is the hallmark of Gauri Power engineering excellence. The regulating columns are precision-wound with 99.9% pure electrolytic grade rectangular copper strips with a minimum cross-sectional area of 24 mm² (expanding up to 180 mm² for 3000–6000 kVA units). A precision linear contact track is CNC-machined along the edge of the winding to accommodate self-lubricating graphite carbon rollers.

The Gauri Power Linear Strip Advantage:

Conventional Market Dimmers: Wound with thin round enameled wire (≤ 4 mm²) using tiny carbon pencil points that slide under friction. They groove the copper within 12–18 months, cause severe sparking, and burn out during motor startup inrush.
Gauri Power Rolling Technology: Massive rectangular copper strips (≥ 24 mm²) with rotating graphite carbon rollers. Rollers distribute current over a continuous contact zone, lasting 8 to 10 years with virtually zero track wear and over 99% efficiency.

STAGE 04 OF 07 Mechanical Assembly & Tanking Bay

Rigid Core-Coil Assembly, Clamping & Heavy-Gauge Tank Fabrication

The active series buck-boost transformer and regulating auto-transformer column are mounted together on heavy structural ISMC steel channels. Calibrated torque wrenches tighten all tie rods and busbar connections. The assembly is lowered into custom-fabricated heavy-gauge mild steel tanks engineered with external corrugated cooling radiator fins to ensure optimum natural oil convection.

Heavy Structural Base: Channel frame designed with bi-directional heavy-duty steel castor wheels for effortless industrial positioning.
Laser-Welded Radiator Banks: Seamless radiator panels maximizing heat dissipation, keeping oil top temperature rise well within 45°C.
Conservator Tank & Dehydrating Breather: Allows oil expansion while silica gel removes 100% of airborne moisture during thermal breathing.
24-Hour Hydraulic Pressure Test: Every tank is tested at 0.5 kg/cm² pressure for 24 hours to guarantee absolute leak-proof integrity.

STAGE 05 OF 07 Thermostatic Baking & Oil Bay

Thermostatic Vacuum Baking (105°C) & De-Aerated Mineral Oil Impregnation

Moisture is the single greatest cause of dielectric breakdown in industrial voltage stabilizers. The complete core-coil assembly is placed in our vacuum drying oven and baked at a thermostatically controlled temperature of 105°C under deep vacuum (< 0.1 mbar) until moisture is completely extracted from the insulation fibers. Virgin transformer-grade mineral oil is then filled directly under vacuum.

Deep Vacuum Moisture Extraction: Purges residual humidity from cellulose pressboard pores, elevating insulation resistance (≥ 1000 MΩ).
Micro-Bubble Elimination: Filling oil under vacuum prevents trapped air pockets between winding layers, eliminating partial discharge and corona.
Virgin Mineral Oil BDV > 60 kV: Tested in-house as per IS 335 / IEC 60296 with moisture content verified under 15 PPM.

STAGE 06 OF 07 Electronics Calibration & Testing Bay

Microcontroller AVR Card Calibration & 100% In-House Routine Testing (IS 9815)

Prior to dispatch approval, the stabilizer's digital microcontroller Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR) card is integrated and calibrated. Every unit undergoes 100% routine electrical and performance tests in our computerized test bay in strict compliance with IS 9815 and IS 2026.

Regulation Accuracy & Speed Test: Verified under simulated fluctuating supply to hold output steady within ± 1% (or ± 0.5% precision) with response correction speed up to 35 V/sec.
Electronic Safety Cut-Off Checks: Multi-parameter verification of under-voltage trip, over-voltage trip, phase-reversal sensing, single-phasing prevention, and electronic overload timer.
Separate Source High Voltage Withstand: 28 kV / 70 kV power frequency dielectric withstand test applied for 60 seconds without breakdown.
Induced Overvoltage (DVDF): Tested at double rated voltage at 100 Hz / 200 Hz frequency to guarantee turn-to-turn and layer insulation strength.

STAGE 07 OF 07 Surface Finishing & Logistics Bay

7-Tank Chemical Pre-Treatment, Polyurethane Enamel & Freight Dispatch

Industrial stabilizers must endure harsh ambient conditions—ranging from high humidity in coastal states to acidic fumes in chemical plants and abrasive dust in rolling mills. All steel tanks undergo a 7-tank chemical de-greasing, de-rusting, and zinc-phosphating sequence before receiving dual coats of zinc-chromate primer and tough polyurethane (PU) topcoat enamel.

7-Tank Chemical Phosphating: Creates an insoluble zinc-phosphate crystal layer on bare steel, preventing paint flaking and corrosion.
Industrial PU Topcoat (RAL 7032 / 631): High gloss, chemical-resistant finish cured for maximum hardness and UV resistance.
Heavy Crated Packaging & Pan-India Transit: Bolted to wooden skids, moisture-wrapped, and loaded via overhead crane for direct transit to client factory sites.
