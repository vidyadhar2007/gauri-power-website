Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Gauri Power

1. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by clients, industrial plant engineers, dealers, and website visitors when requesting technical quotations (RFQs), registering corporate accounts, or communicating with our Sales Department. This includes contact details, company information, GSTIN numbers, and plant electrical parameters.

2. How We Use Industrial Data

Your technical specifications and electrical single-line diagrams (SLDs) are used solely by Gauri Power' authorized sales engineering staff to evaluate electrical requirements, prepare official commercial quotations, and manufacture compliant servo stabilizer units and supply switchgear. We do not sell or lease proprietary plant drawings or customer information to third parties.

3. Document Security

Uploaded engineering documents (PDF, DWG, DOCX) are stored securely on our dedicated internal servers and accessed only by authorized technical personnel involved in quotation engineering.

4. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding our privacy practices, contact us at: Gauripower09@gmail.com or via written notice to our Ropar Works & Office.
