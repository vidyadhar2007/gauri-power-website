PILLAR 01: DIRECT MANUFACTURING Works: Ropar (Punjab)

In-House Production of Industrial Servo Voltage Stabilizers

At our purpose-built works in Ropar, Punjab, Gauri Power designs and manufactures heavy-duty Automatic Servo Voltage Stabilizers engineered specifically for demanding Indian grid conditions. While mass-market brands use thin round copper wire and fragile carbon pencils that burn out under motor inrush, we build exclusively for extreme continuous duty.

Rectangular Copper Strip Winding

Wound with 99.9% electrolytic copper strip with a minimum cross-section of ≥ 24 mm², handling massive motor startup inrushes without thermal rise.

Graphite Carbon Roller Mechanism

Self-lubricating graphite carbon rollers that roll smoothly across machined copper tracks, ensuring an 8 to 10 year maintenance-free lifespan without grooving.

Prime CRGO Step-Lap Stacking

100% prime laser-scribed silicon steel cut at 45° mitred angles, keeping magnetic flux density below 1.65 Tesla and humming below 50 dB.

Advanced Digital AVR Controllers

High-speed micro-controller sensing circuits delivering ± 1% regulation accuracy, 10-35 V/sec correction, and full under/over voltage trip protection.

PILLAR 02: AUTHORIZED DEALERSHIP & SUPPLY Pan-India Substation Delivery

High-Voltage Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCB) & Switchgear Solutions

To provide clients with comprehensive electrical infrastructure, Gauri Power operates as an authorized commercial dealer and turnkey supplier of High-Voltage Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCB). We supply factory-certified 11 kV and 33 kV indoor switchgear panels, compact RMU units, and substation draw-out breakers engineered for rapid fault clearance, zero oil fire hazards, and reliable operation.

Ultra-Fast Fault Interruption (< 50 ms)

Hermetically sealed ceramic vacuum bottles extinguish arcs within milliseconds during high short-circuit events (up to 40 kA breaking capacity).

Compact RMU & Indoor Metal-Clad Panels

Fully compartmentalized IP4X metal-clad indoor panels and IP67 hermetic Ring Main Units (RMU) with motorized draw-out racking for modern plant switchgear rooms.

Class M2 Mechanical Endurance

Motorized spring charging mechanism tested for over 10,000 operations without mechanical failure, ideal for frequent auto-reclosing duty.

Numerical Protection & SCADA Ready

Integrated microprocessor numerical relays (overcurrent, earth fault, instantaneous trip) with RS485 / Modbus communication for plant automation.
