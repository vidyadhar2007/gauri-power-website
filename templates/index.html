Engineering Excellence

7-Stage Precision Manufacturing Journey

From raw electrolytic copper strip winding and vertical linear carbon rollers to microcontroller AVR calibration and 100% dielectric routine testing at our Ropar works.

STAGE 01

Computerized Design

Custom electrical parameter calculation, thermal gradient modeling, and harmonic mitigation engineering tailored to customer requirements.

STAGE 02

CRGO Core Stacking

Precision mitred cutting and step-lap joint stacking of prime grade Cold Rolled Grain Oriented (CRGO) silicon steel to minimize core losses.

STAGE 03

Coil Winding

Heavy rectangular section electrolytic copper strips wound under controlled tension for short-circuit withstand integrity.

STAGE 04

Core-Coil Assembly

Rigid mechanical clamping, lead connection brazing, and fitment into steel tanks with heavy corrugated radiators for thermal dissipation.

STAGE 05

Vacuum & Oven Drying

Vacuum impregnated and oven dried as per International Standards, eliminating all moisture from cellulose insulation.

STAGE 06

Dielectric & HV Testing

Rigorous testing: High Voltage Breakdown, Insulation Resistance, Ratio & Polarity, and No-Load / Full-Load Loss tests.

STAGE 07

Quality Inspection

Independent QA clearance, inspection documentation, paint thickness checks, and issuance of factory test certificates.

DISPATCH

Protected Dispatch

Secure transit packaging with unidirectional castor wheels, nitrogen blanketing for large units, and direct factory freight.
